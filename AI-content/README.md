# Eigenism — a short film

A two-minute visual explanation of [Eigenism](https://eigenism.org/paper.pdf) (Dan Hendrycks), written for a middle-school reading level.

Narrated, with a score. The pictures carry the idea and the voice explains it:

**You are not just a body. You are a pattern** — memories, friends, the way you see the world. An AI can copy, pause, and split, so “is that still me?” is not yes or no. It is *how much*.

Care = closeness × happiness. Family carries pieces of you. If an AI shares a real life with people, protecting them becomes protecting itself.

Twelve shots, about 1:45. Each shot holds until its line finishes, so the
picture never cuts a sentence in half. The voiceover and the music bed were
generated with ElevenLabs and live in `media/audio/`; [SCRIPT.md](SCRIPT.md) has
the full narration with timecodes.

Open [index.html](index.html) to watch. Sound is on once you press play — the
♪ button (or `m`) mutes it.
