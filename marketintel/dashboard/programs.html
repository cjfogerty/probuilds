Metric definitions & honesty notes
  • Category: from site.category when present; else derived from brand/program/name/session keywords (ninja → martial → cheer/dance → adventure → STEM → gymnastics → other).
  • Sites for enroll %: sites where enrolled != null (includes explicit 0).
  • Avg enrollment: sum(enrolled) / sites_with_enroll.
  • Avg price: mean(price_low) among sites with price; Urban Air excluded from revenue/avg-price paths (jump-pass).
  • Est monthly: for monthly billing: enrolled × price_low (non-UA). Per-lesson: not blind-annualized.
  • Est annual: monthly × 12 when monthly billing is solid; otherwise weak / —.
  • Pull week is the intel scrape window, not session calendar dates.
  • Summary by only regroups the enrollment bar chart — the site table always lists individual filtered sites.
  • Program = program_name || program || brand.
