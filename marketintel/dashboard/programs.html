Metric definitions & honesty notes
  • Sites for enroll %: sites where enrolled != null (includes explicit 0).
  • Avg enrollment: sum(enrolled) / sites_with_enroll.
  • Avg price: mean(price_low) among sites with price; Urban Air excluded from revenue/avg-price paths (jump-pass).
  • Est monthly: for billing_interval === monthly: enrolled × price_low (when both present, non-UA). For per_lesson/each: only annualize via assumed lessons/mo when n_classes suggests a rate; otherwise mark partial — show list×enrolled with unit label, do not fake annualize.
  • Est annual: monthly × 12 when monthly billing is solid; otherwise weak / partial / —.
  • Pull week (week_start–week_end) is the intel scrape window, not session calendar dates.
  • Program = program || program_name || brand; badge shown when falling back to brand (no program field in schema yet).
