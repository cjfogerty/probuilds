Enrollment coverage by category

The universe is platform-visible supply, not the market. A site is here because its operator publishes class availability through Unleashed, iClassPro, Jackrabbit, Pike13 or My Gym. An independent on paper scheduling is invisible. So every share figure is an upper bound, every penetration figure is a lower bound, and a metro showing very few sites is more likely a hole in the data than a hole in the market — which is why those rows are separated out rather than ranked as opportunity.
